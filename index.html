Ānvīkṣikī—reasoned inquiry—is regarded as a lamp for all learning, a means for every undertaking, and a support for all duties.

Anvikshalaya takes inspiration from this idea of inquiry and illumination. The studio name itself is not a word from the verse.

Sprout

A little growth. Every day.

Every habit is a little plant. Each check-in helps it grow, and each week leaves a pot on your shelf. Miss a day and nothing dies or resets; the next week brings a fresh seed.

01 / GROW

A little sunlight, earned.

Check-ins earn Sunlight to spend on seeds and painted scenes.

02 / KEEP

A pot that turns to gold.

Every check-in counts toward 66, across every week.

Moonrail

Your focus has a window seat.

A cozy pixel focus timer for your Mac’s menu bar. The moon crosses the sky as your session goes on. Arrive at a station, hear a soft chime, and take a breather with Momo.

Find your quiet rhythm.

Focus sessions and station breaks, with a little train for company.

02 / REMEMBER

Every mile, in the logbook.

Look back at your focus by day, week, and month.
